    chapter 1

    1 · The Quiet Night

    Once, on the quietest night ever, a little ghost named Wisp couldn’t fall asleep. Nobody hummed. Nobody sang. And the dream that was meant for Wisp… just floated away.

    2 · The Falling Dream

    That dream was me! I drifted past the moon for a long, long time. Then I bonked into a chimney. Ow. Two little stars got stuck in my eyes on the way down. (I kept them. They’re nice.)

    3 · The Lost-and-Found Basket

    I landed in the Midnight Nursery’s lost-and-found, between a sock and a very grumpy mitten. Nana Noctua found me, tucked a nightcap on my head, and said, “Every lost thing has a job here, little one.”

    4 · Too Quiet

    But nights were so quiet. I did not like it one bit. So I hummed. It was wobbly. It was off-key. A cricket covered its ears. But the baby moths next door… fell asleep!

    ♪ turn sound on to hear me hum

    5 · The First Real Dream

    I remembered Wisp. So I built my very first dream: one lantern, one lullaby, and lots of trying again. Wisp yawned. Wisp smiled. Wisp slept. I giggled so hard I fell off my cloud.

    6 · Junior Dream Engineer

    Now I work at the Midnight Nursery. Nobody here has a silent, dreamless night anymore. Not on my shift.

    I build the dreams you fall asleep inside.
